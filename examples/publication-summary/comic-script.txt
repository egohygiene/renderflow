THE LANTERN MAP
Original synthetic comic script; text companion only.

Panel 1. Pip, an orange circle courier, reaches a foggy pier carrying a lantern. Vale, a blue square mapmaker, unfolds a paper map beside a closed ferry gate.
Pip: "The light arrived. Did the route?"
Vale: "Only as far as this pier."

Panel 2. They hold the map under the lantern. A penciled route stops at the shoreline; no crossing is drawn.
Pip: "Then we mark what we know."
Vale draws a small dot at the pier. They wait beside the gate.
